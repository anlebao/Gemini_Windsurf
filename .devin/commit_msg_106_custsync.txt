fix(#106): TD-CUSTSYNC-001 — customer identity merge + SQLite→PG sync

Root cause: Guest checkout creates DeviceId-based customer stubs in
SQLite. When customer logs in (Google OAuth / Phone OTP), a NEW
customer record is created with different Id. Loyalty points awarded
to DeviceId stubs are invisible to login account → /my-loyalty shows 0.

Phase 1 — DeviceId→Login Merge (fixes #106 directly):
- New ICustomerMergeService + CustomerMergeService
- MergeDeviceStubsIntoLoginAsync: finds all active customers with
  matching DeviceId, sums their LoyaltyRewards.PointBalance into
  login customer, concatenates history, soft-deletes stubs, links
  DeviceId to login customer
- SocialAuthController.GoogleCallback: extract device_token from
  state param, call merge after customer found/created
- CustomerIdentityController.VerifyOtp: call merge with request.DeviceId
- Login.razor: pass customer_device_id as state param to Google OAuth

Phase 2 — Auto-link DeviceId on login (prevents future fragmentation):
- CustomerMergeService links DeviceId to login customer after merge
- Checkout.razor already passes CustomerId when logged in + validated
- Future guest checkouts on same device will find login customer
  via DeviceId (no new stub created)

Phase 3 — SQLite→PG Customer Sync (closes TD-CUSTSYNC-001):
- DataSyncSubscriber.SyncCustomerCreatedAsync: complete full upsert
  (was deferred — only checked existence, never inserted)
- OrderWorkflowService.CreateCustomerStubAsync: enqueue CustomerCreated
  outbox event → NatsSyncWorker → NATS → Gateway DataSyncSubscriber
  upserts customer to PostgreSQL
