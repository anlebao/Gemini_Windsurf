fix(tests): use VanAnDbContext (superset) for EnsureCreated in test factories

8 W5/W7 accounting tests failed with 'no such table: JournalEntries' during
SeedVasDataAsync. Root cause: CustomWebApplicationFactory and
AuthRealWebApplicationFactory called EnsureCreated on ShopERPDbContext only.
ShopERPDbContext excludes accounting entities (JournalEntries, AccountCharts,
AccountingEntries, etc.) — its EnsureCreated never creates those tables.
VanAnDbContext.EnsureCreatedAsync() in tests was a no-op (database already
exists from ShopERPDbContext.EnsureCreated).

Fix: Call EnsureCreated on VanAnDbContext instead of ShopERPDbContext.
VanAnDbContext is the SUPERSET model — includes both business AND accounting
entities. Its EnsureCreated creates ALL tables. ShopERPDbContext shares the
same SQLite connection and its tables are a subset, so no separate
EnsureCreated is needed.

Also changed IsSchemaCreated() parameter type from ShopERPDbContext to
DbContext (base type) so both contexts can use it.

Result: 23 failed -> 15 failed (+8 tests passing, no regressions).
All 9 VasReportsEndpointTests now PASS.
