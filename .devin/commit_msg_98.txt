  KhachLink OrderTracking page chỉ nhận update qua 15s polling → khi owner
  bấm "Sẵn sàng giao" / "Hoàn tất", khách hàng thấy status cũ (pending)
  trong tối đa 15s trước khi poll fetch status mới.
- Fix 1 (Gateway): OrderNotificationService now also broadcasts
  "OrderStatusUpdated" event to LocationHub "order_{orderId}" group.
  KhachLink customers already join this group via JoinOrderTracking.
- Fix 2 (KhachLink): OrderTracking.razor adds _hubConnection.On<
  object>("OrderStatusUpdated") handler. Parses payload, updates
  _orderStatus + _statusSteps + StateHasChanged immediately.
